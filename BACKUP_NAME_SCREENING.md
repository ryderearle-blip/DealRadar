This is a preliminary knockout search, not a legal clearance opinion. It is meant to give trademark counsel a short, practical list to investigate. It does not establish that a name is registrable or safe to use.

## Names removed from consideration

These names showed an existing or closely related commercial use during the knockout search: TrovePath, ShopRove, CartCove, BuyVela, Navilo, ShopRaze, Nearlii, CartMint, Pricelyt, Pickora, and ValoRaid.

## Search method and limitations

- Exact-word searches were run in the official USPTO Trademark Search system.
- General web searches checked for obvious existing shopping, price-comparison, app, and retail uses.
- `.com` checks used the Verisign RDAP service.
- The search did not fully cover confusingly similar spellings, sound-alikes, design marks, state registrations, foreign registrations, company-name databases, app stores, social handles, or unregistered common-law rights.

## Questions for trademark counsel

Ask counsel to review DealRadar and the four candidates for:

1. Similar-looking and similar-sounding marks, not only exact matches.
2. Relevant goods and services in Classes 9, 35, and 42.
3. U.S. federal and state records, common-law use, app stores, business registries, and important international markets.
4. Whether any wording is too descriptive to receive strong protection.
5. The best filing owner, first-use strategy, and filing basis.

Official starting points: [USPTO Trademark Search](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) and [USPTO comprehensive clearance guidance](https://www.uspto.gov/trademarks/search/comprehensive-clearance-search-similar-trademarks).
